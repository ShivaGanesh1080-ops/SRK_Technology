# SRK Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
SRK (Sri Radha Krishna Pvt. Ltd.) is an early-stage education and technology skill-development venture. The objective is to build a platform that connects students, colleges, trainers, and industry. 

## 2. Core Objectives
1. **Public Brand Website:** Establish SRK as a premium, reliable technology training partner.
2. **Student Workshop Discovery & Registration:** Allow students to find and enroll in workshops.
3. **College Partnership System:** Let colleges request customized workshops or training programs.
4. **Future-Ready Platform:** Lay the foundation for a scalable multi-college ecosystem.

## 3. User Personas
- **Students:** Seeking practical, hands-on learning experiences and certificates to build their portfolio.
- **Colleges:** Seeking reliable tech partners to train their students on emerging technologies.
- **Trainers:** Industry professionals teaching practical skills.
- **Administrators:** Managing the platform, tracking metrics, and processing feedback.

## 4. MVP (Milestone 1 & 2) Features
- Landing page with story-driven narrative.
- Workshops browsing and domain exploration.
- Student authentication and registration.
- College partnership inquiry form.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Fast initial load, optimized images.
- **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first, functioning well on all devices.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation.
- **Security:** Secure authentication, data validation, no exposed secrets.
